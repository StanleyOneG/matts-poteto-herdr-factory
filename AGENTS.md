## Important note to know while working with Matt Pocock's skills

Because of the names collision of skills `tdd` and `teack` between Matt Pocock's skills in `./.agents/skills/` and `@zenspc/pi-pstack` Pi extension currently installed globally (`~/.pi/agent/settings.json`), I explicitly renamed these skills in `./.agents/skills/` to `matt-tdd` and `matt-teach`, and also respectively edited mentions of them in other Matt's skills.
